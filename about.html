About daren.gg

Building the next generation of League of Legends tools for Mac.

Our mission

daren.gg is dedicated to creating the best League of Legends experience for Mac users. We believe macOS players deserve tools that are just as powerful and performant as those on other platforms, without compromising on design or user experience.

Why Mac?

For too long, League of Legends players on Mac have been overlooked. Most gaming tools are built primarily for Windows, with Mac support being an afterthought, if it exists at all.

We're changing that. By building natively for macOS and leveraging Apple's APIs, we're creating tools that feel at home on your Mac while delivering the performance and features you need to improve your gameplay.

What we're building

Performance overlay

Our flagship product is a real-time overlay that provides champion insights, build recommendations, and performance analytics, all while keeping system resource usage minimal thanks to native macOS integration.

Player profiles

Track your progress, analyze your matches, and find areas for improvement with detailed player profiles and statistics. Coming soon.

Open source

We believe in transparency and community collaboration. Our web platform and future tools are open source, so the community can contribute, audit, and improve the tools they use every day.
